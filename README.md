# Tiny-Italian-Spanish-Dataset
UPDATE: The dataset was removed from public access due to misuse concerns, however feel free to reach out to me through discord (@gianloop). Alternatively, you can fill out this questionnaire, however it might take me longer to give out the dataset: https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdDej5fCuVy1G1CfGVvUu7oz1kaAr0gpcLhJ2_52XLHd416Ig/viewform?usp=header

A small dataset with Italian and Spanish singing data, labeled in HTK format.

The dataset is approx. 32 minutes of data, comprised of 24 minutes of Italian singing and 8 minutes of Spanish singing.

Some of the data is inconsistent in quality and may not provide optimal results.

The data is in 16 bit WAV format, 44.1 KHZ and mono.
